Essential Terminology
Terms every payments professional must know — and own
RTGS
Real-Time Gross Settlement — each payment settled individually, in real time, with finality. No netting. Used for high-value urgent payments (Fedwire, TARGET2, CHAPS).
Nostro / Vostro
Nostro = "our" account held at another bank. Vostro = "their" account held at our bank. Same account, different perspective — critical for cross-border payments.
BIC / IBAN
BIC (Bank Identifier Code) = identifies the bank. IBAN = identifies the account. Both required for international transfers. ABA routing number used in the US.
Correspondent Banking
A bank that processes payments on behalf of another bank in a given currency/geography. Each hop adds time, cost, and potential risk.
Intraday Liquidity
Available funds to meet payment obligations throughout the day. Banks must manage liquidity across multiple currencies and time zones simultaneously.
Cut-off Time
The deadline by which payment instructions must be submitted for same-day processing. Missing cut-off = next business day processing. Different per rail and geography.
💡 Trainer: Nostro/Vostro always confuses delegates. Use whiteboard to draw it: "Same €10M, two banks, two perspectives." Then ask: "Who funds the Nostro account?" — answer: the originating bank.

Seven stages every payment passes through — from instruction to confirmation

2

🔴 Where Exceptions Occur Most
Validation (wrong/missing data) · Clearing (network reject) · Settlement (liquidity shortfall) · Reconciliation (unmatched items)
⚡ Time-Critical Zone
Stages 2–4 (Validation → Routing) must complete within milliseconds for RTP. For SWIFT, this takes minutes to hours across correspondents.

:20:
Transaction Reference
Unique identifier — used for tracking and investigation
:32A:
Value Date, Currency & Amount
YYMMDD + ISO currency code + amount. Critical for settlement timing.
:50K:
Ordering Customer
The entity instructing the payment — name and address required
:57A:
Beneficiary Institution
The bank holding the beneficiary's account — identified by BIC
:59:
Beneficiary
Account number (IBAN) + name + address of the payment recipient
:70:
Remittance Information
Invoice reference, payment purpose — key for reconciliation
:71A:
⚠ Charge Bearer — High Impact Field
OUR = sender pays all fees · SHA = shared (most common) · BEN = beneficiary pays all. Disputes arise when client expects OUR but gets SHA.

Session 03 · Key Takeaways
SWIFT & ISO 20022 — what we covered
1
SWIFT is a Messaging Network
Not a settlement system. SWIFT sends the instruction — central bank RTGS systems (Fedwire, TARGET2, CHAPS) settle the actual funds.
2
MT103 Field Mastery
Fields :20:, :32A:, :50K:, :57A:, :59:, :70:, :71A: — every payments professional must know these. Field :71A: is the most disputed.
3
ISO 20022 is the Future — Now
Richer XML data, 2,000+ fields vs MT's ~200. SWIFT cross-border migration is active. SEPA & Fedwire already use it. Act on readiness now.
4
Richer Data = Smarter Operations
ISO 20022 enables better fraud detection, faster investigations, automated reconciliation — and feeds AI/ML systems with consistent structured data.

Session 04 · Key Takeaways
Exception Handling & Operations — what we covered
1
Exceptions Are Inevitable
Every payment operation encounters exceptions. The differentiator is speed, accuracy, and consistency of resolution — not prevention alone.
2
The Six-Stage Workflow
Fail → Capture → Investigate → Decision → Repair → Confirm. Document every step. SLAs are the discipline that keeps operations accountable.
3
Reconciliation is Control
Daily MT940/camt.053 reconciliation ensures every transaction is accounted for. 99% auto-match rate should be the target — aged items must escalate.
4
gpi Changed Tracking
SWIFT gpi's UETR gives end-to-end payment visibility. Clients expect real-time status — banks that provide this reduce query volumes by 30–40%.

Future of Payments — Six Key Trends
Forces reshaping the industry over the next three to five years
⚡
NOW
Real-Time Payments Everywhere
FedNow (US, 2023), PIX (Brazil), UPI (India), FAST (Singapore). 24/7 instant settlement is becoming the global baseline. Batch processing is declining for many use cases.
🔗
2025–26
Open Banking 2.0
Moving from read-only data access to active payment initiation. Variable Recurring Payments (VRP) and embedded finance are transforming how businesses pay and collect.
☁
ONGOING
Cloud-Native Core Banking
Banks migrating from monolithic legacy systems to cloud-native microservices. Enables faster product launches, elastic scaling, and dramatically improved resilience.
🤖
NOW
AI & ML in Payments
Fraud detection, AML screening, exception handling, reconciliation — all being transformed by ML. AI augments human judgment; it does not replace expertise.
📋
2025–26
ISO 20022 Ubiquity
Full SWIFT migration + Fedwire + CHAPS + SEPA. 2,000+ structured fields enable new services: smart routing, purpose-coded payments, automated reconciliation.
💎
2026+
CBDCs & Tokenised Money
Central Bank Digital Currencies and tokenised deposits moving from pilot to reality. Could reshape interbank settlement and cross-border payments at the foundation level.
